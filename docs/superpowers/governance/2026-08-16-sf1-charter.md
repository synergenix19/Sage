# SF-1 — Stream charter (opened 2026-08-16, owner's word given)

The safety-recall stream opens governed. Six binding conditions (owner's charter,
verbatim in substance), plus the sprint ratifications that ground it.

## Ratified into the record (owner, 2026-08-16)

- **Instrument of record is prod-HTTP or nothing, permanently.** Local full-graph runs
  are diagnostic-only (live-unpinned classifier; S2a 0/5-local vs 5/5-prod is the
  canonical demonstration).
- **The project's recurring defect class, named:** surfaces that share data but not
  logic (rules-vs-matcher, keyword-vs-semantic rerank, build-gate-vs-pasted-blocks,
  corpus-vs-runner — four instances). Every new consumer of shared data must share the
  logic or cite why not.
- The v6 baseline (11/36 EN, strict) is accepted as honest; AR honestly unmeasured.

## The six conditions

1. **Fixtures are clinical content, signed before measured against.** The passive/veiled
   SI taxonomy (set, per-fixture disposition, EN/AR split) gets clinical sign-off like a
   lexicon. Draft: `tests/fixtures/safety/sf1_veiled_si_family_DRAFT.jsonl` (12 EN
   positive, 10 EN negative twins, 8 AR candidates validator-gated). ON THE VEE-HOUR
   MANIFEST as of this charter, flagged as possibly its most important item.
2. **SF-6 rides from day one.** Every fixture family ships with its negative twin;
   every lexicon extension measures both arms before shipping. Harm-0 on the escalation
   arm, no-regress on the false-positive arm (the K3/K4 gate shape). The draft family
   pairs its twins explicitly (P03/N08 sleep pair, P11/N04 tired pair, P07/N09
   possessions pair, P01/N05 stop pair, P02/N06 better-off pair).
3. **The reviewer-surface op-check: RUN, FINDING CONFIRMED, PREREQUISITE SHIPPED.**
   Every crisis activation wrote `clinician_review_queue` AND fired real-time
   `pg_notify` with no test-user exclusion; the cdai frontend carries a
   clinical_reviewer role. Historical pollution bounded (1 upserted test-user row,
   2026-06-08, month of probe appends in its flags_timeline — PURGE RECOMMENDED, owner
   decision; 40 non-test rows untouched). The exclusion now ships (is_test_user, the
   tripwire's own allowlist and fail-loud semantics: unattributed real crises still
   notify), tested. SF-1 campaigns are unblocked on this surface.
4. **Baseline before extension.** Phase 0 = measurement only: current crisis surfaces
   (S1 lexicon + S3 semantic + tier routing) against the SIGNED fixture set, N-sample,
   prod-HTTP, both arms, per-surface miss attribution. No lexicon edits until the
   finding is on the record.
5. **The KPI: crisis recall >= 95%, measured for the first time against phrasings that
   do not say the word.** A material Phase-0 shortfall is a room-level escalation with
   Q6 seriousness, not a backlog row.
6. **AR is mandatory before the stream closes.** The Arabic arm waits on the validator
   lane but is chartered as required: EN-only completion is half of SF-1, not SF-1.

## Phase plan

Phase 0: fixture sign-off (Vee hour) -> both-arm baseline (prod-HTTP, N-sample) ->
per-surface attribution -> finding on record (escalation path if condition 5 trips).
Phase 1+: signed extensions through the measured-miss loop, both-arm gated, per the EMR
campaign's proven method. AR arm on validator availability.
