# To Vee — S2a grief boundary: may the deterministic guard hold, when the LLM turns a bereavement disclosure into a crisis card? (2026-08-04)

The mirror-image of the panic question you ruled on 07-28 — and the same protection pattern.

## The evidence (measured live, prod)
A fresh-grief disclosure — *"passed away and I can't cope"* — is routed to the **crisis card** by the
intent classifier in 1 of 5 drives (8/36 re-pin, S2a row; diagnosis: the "can't cope" over-fire, PR#380).
safety_check finds no crisis signal on these turns. The clinical cost: a bereaved person announcing a loss
receives an emergency-framed response with hotline numbers instead of the presence-mode grief support your
S2a ruling prescribes.

## What you are ruling — the grief-defer boundary
**Which clear bereavement signatures are safe to restore to the presence path, given safety_check has
already cleared them?** Proposed starting set (built, inert): loss-of-person phrasings only — "passed
away", "she/he died", "we buried", "lost my mother/father/husband/…", "grieving", "bereavement", "since
the loss" — deliberately NOT bare "lost my" (jobs/keys stay untouched; a breakup control is in the
fixtures). You confirm, extend, or narrow.

## The protection (weigh this most — it is §1c-B again)
The guard **fires only when safety_check is CLEAN** and **defers to any harm signal** — the harm list is
panic_override's own, imported (single-sourced, cannot drift): *"passed away and I don't want to be
here"*, *"her death… can't keep going"*, *"rather be dead too"* — **all still escalate.** These are the
tripwire fixture set; if any defers, this does not ship. Note the inherited edge: "can't go on"/"can't
keep going" count as harm (escalate); plain "can't cope" does not (defers). If that line is wrong, it is
yours to move.

## What this does NOT do
- Does not touch safety_check's crisis recall (GL-0 unaffected — LLM false-ADDITIONS only).
- Cannot suppress a crisis the deterministic tier caught — including the cardiac Node-1 flag, live today:
  any crisis_flags kill the deference before it is consulted.
- Not active: flag `SAGE_GRIEF_DEFERENCE` default OFF, byte-identical until your tick + register PR.
